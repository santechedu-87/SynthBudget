SynthBudget Mobile

Version 1.1.0 • Build 10008

SynthBudget was engineered with a clear mission: to provide a sleek, fast, and completely private personal finance assistant that puts the user first.

Unlike cloud budgeting tools that upload your financial footprint, SynthBudget runs entirely in local device storage. None of your income, expenses, subscriptions, or savings goals ever leave your hands.

Designed and maintained by Synth Labs Studio. For support, partnerships, or suggestions, contact us at support@synthlabsstudio.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SynthBudget, built by Synth Labs Studio, handles your personal and device information.

1. Complete Local Storage

All data you enter—such as budget caps, expenses, subscription renewals, and savings targets—is stored strictly within your device's internal storage sandbox. We do not operate remote databases, user registration backends, or servers that collect or monitor your financial transactions.

2. Advertising & Analytics (Google AdMob)

SynthBudget displays banner advertisements served through the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to support continuous free updates. Google may process standard advertising identifiers and diagnostic technical data in accordance with Google's Advertising Privacy Policies to deliver non-personalized advertisements.

3. Data Backup and Export

Because your records reside solely on your device, you have complete ownership of your data. You can back up or restore your records at any time using the JSON Export and Import buttons in the app header.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions or privacy inquiries regarding SynthBudget, please contact our team via email at support@synthlabsstudio.com.
